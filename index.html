    A few helpful answers

    What does the interval between buses mean?

    Also called headway, it is the time between consecutive buses passing the same stop in the same direction. The target can vary by route and operating period.

    Are these actual bus arrival times?

    No. This student prototype uses fictional routes, times and incidents to demonstrate the proposed workflow. It is not connected to Rapid KL operations.

    Who can use the service dashboard?

    The concept is designed for operations controllers, terminal dispatchers and depot supervisors. You can explore the sample data, then use a demo staff account to try operational actions.

    Does a replacement request change an arrival time?

    No. A request must be reviewed, and vehicle availability must be confirmed. The forecast stays unchanged until there is a confirmed operational update.

    How can I try the staff login?

    Select Staff login, choose Controller or Supervisor, and select Fill demo credentials. These accounts only demonstrate the interface; they do not provide access to a real staff system.
